About

I build embedded systems and flight sim hardware from whatever is available. No 3D printer. No CNC. No fresh materials. Just workshop scrap, hand tools, and microcontrollers that cost less than a cup of coffee.

Every project is a full cycle — design, carve, solder, code, test, document. Firmware in STM32 HAL and Arduino. Interfaces that run in the browser with Web Serial. All open source.
