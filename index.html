PROJECT 001
TBAS
Tactical Battlefield Awareness Suite — acoustic direction finding using TDOA physics. Two microphones 28cm apart calculate sound angle, a servo tracks the source, and a rolling-code IR system identifies friend or foe. Live browser radar over Web Serial.

PROJECT 002
HOTAS_JOYSTICK
DIY HOTAS sidestick — hand-carved from furniture scrap destined for bonfire, painted with leftover house paint. STM32 Blue Pill running native USB HID. No drivers, no vJoy. 4 axes, 10 buttons, 100Hz polling. Tested in FlightGear and War Thunder.

PROJECT 003
DIY-ROUGH-GAMEPAD
Handmade USB HID gamepad and reusable test pad — STM32F103C8T6, 6 analog axes, 12 buttons, no drivers needed. Built to prove you can make a working game controller from rough, repurposed parts.
